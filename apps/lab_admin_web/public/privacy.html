Privacy Policy for Mobile Application

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

At Shwe Health ("we", "us", "our"), we are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of your personal and medical information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal health or medical information with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide healthcare diagnostics, sample collection scheduling, and result reporting services. The types of information we collect include:

  • Personal Identity Information: Your full name, email address, phone number, and account credentials when you register.
  • Medical & Health Data: Prescription photos/uploads, laboratory test orders, results, and medical history shared for processing diagnostics.
  • Location Information: Real-time or coarse geolocations (latitude and longitude) used to pinpoint your home or clinic address on the map when scheduling a sample collection.
  • Camera & Photo Access: Permissions required to capture and upload a photo of your prescription directly to the lab.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for medical and operational purposes, including to:

  • Facilitate, verify, and complete laboratory test orders.
  • Schedule and direct medical collectors to your location for sample extraction.
  • Securely deliver official laboratory test results and PDF reports directly to your device.
  • Offer helpful AI-assisted diagnostic report explanations using secure, health-compliant processing.
  • Manage and track your customer loyalty points.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

To deliver our healthcare services, we share your data only with trusted partners as follows:

  • Diagnostic Laboratories: To process and verify your orders and run clinical tests.
  • Medical Collectors: To coordinate at-home or clinic-based sample extraction (using your name, phone number, and map location pin).
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, regulation, or legal subpoena.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement strict, industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security safeguards to secure your private health data. All medical transmissions and laboratory reports are encrypted in transit and at rest. Your data is stored on secure servers with restricted access controls.

5. Your Rights & Choice

You have full control over your personal and health information. You have the right to:

  • Access, review, and edit your profile or contact details directly in the App.
  • Revoke app permissions at any time (such as Camera or Location tracking) through your device's system settings.
  • Request the complete deletion of your account and personal history from our active servers by contacting us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the revised policy directly in the App or on our hosted website.
